## Design Specifications

### VYBE Logo Design:
- **Symbol**: Bold, geometric "V" letter
- **Background**: Dark navy (#1A237E) with violet-to-cyan gradient
- **Colors**: Violet (#9C27B0) to Cyan (#00BCD4) gradient
- **Foreground**: White (#FFFFFF) symbol
- **Round Variant**: Blue-to-pink-to-green gradient

### Adaptive Icon Features:
- **Background Layer**: Gradient background that shows through
- **Foreground Layer**: Bold V symbol with proper scaling
- **Monochrome Layer**: Simple V for monochrome theme
- **Round Variants**: Separate round versions for Android round icons
